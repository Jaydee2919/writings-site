Why do we apologise for thinking out loud?

Somewhere along the way I learned to pre-apologise for ideas. "This might be wrong but—" "I haven't fully thought this through, but—". What is that? A defensive crouch against being caught uncertain. But uncertainty is the beginning of thought, not its failure.

May 18

The tyranny of the 5-star rating

We've flattened the entire spectrum of human experience into five stars. A film that changed your life: 5 stars. A meal that was fine: 3.5 stars. The infrastructure of judgment has colonised everything, including how we experience the things we judge.

May 9

Cities as personalities

Every city has a theory of the good life baked into its architecture. Mumbai says: density is energy. Tokyo says: precision is care. Barcelona says: the street is a room. The urban planner is an unwitting philosopher.

Apr 30

Against "content"

When did we start calling everything "content"? A novel is content. A symphony is content. A handwritten letter is content. The word flattens the medium, erasing what makes each form distinct. It is the vocabulary of distribution masquerading as the vocabulary of creation.

Apr 22

What would "enough" look like?

I keep trying to answer this question and keep finding it slippery. Enough money. Enough recognition. Enough time. The horizon shifts every time I think I'm close. Maybe that's the point — the pursuit, not the threshold. Or maybe I've just been trained to believe that.
